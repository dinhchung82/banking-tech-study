About this study

I am Vu Dinh Chung — an IT Director / Head of IT with 20+ years in banking and financial services in Vietnam. These notes are how I think about the problems I keep meeting: identity, architecture, delivery, operations, resilience, compliance and people. They are research notes, not a product pitch.
