Glossary

Short definitions for terms used across the study.

← Back to study
TermDefinition
eKYCElectronic Know Your Customer — remote identity verification and onboarding using documents, biometrics and liveness checks.
VNeIDVietnam's national electronic identification account, tied to the citizen identity database; used for authentication and data sharing.
Liveness detectionAnti-spoofing check that confirms a live person (not a photo, video or mask) is present during biometric capture.
CIFCustomer Information File — the core banking record that should be the single source of truth for customer identity.
BCP / DRBusiness Continuity Plan / Disaster Recovery — plans and tested procedures to keep services running and restore them after disruption.
RTO / RPORecovery Time Objective (how fast service must be restored) / Recovery Point Objective (how much data loss is acceptable).
MTTRMean Time To Repair — average time to restore service after an incident; a key operations metric.
SLA / SLOService Level Agreement (contractual target) / Service Level Objective (internal target used to manage toward the SLA).
ITILIT service management framework: incident, problem, change, capacity and service-level management practices.
PCI DSSPayment Card Industry Data Security Standard; the scope (systems touching cardholder data) and how it is contained matter more than the certificate.
ATTT cấp độ (3–4)Vietnam's information-security level classification for information systems under Decree 85/2016/ND-CP; level 3–4 is typical for banking systems.
FinOpsOperating discipline for cloud cost visibility, allocation, optimisation and governance.
IaCInfrastructure as Code — managing infrastructure through versioned, reviewable definitions (Terraform, Ansible).
ESB / API gatewayIntegration middleware that mediates communication between core systems, channels and partners.
BPMNBusiness Process Model and Notation — a standard way to model business processes before building them.
DevSecOpsDelivery model that builds security and operations into CI/CD rather than adding them as a final gate.
Straight-through rateShare of onboarding/transaction cases completed without manual intervention; a design outcome, not luck.
